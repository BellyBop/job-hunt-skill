# Example cover letter (for tone and structure)

Generated cover letters copy the tone and shape of this one. Replace it with a letter of your own that you like, or keep this structure.

---

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in the [role] position at [company]. [One sentence on your background and why this specific role appeals.]

Here is what caught my eye about your job posting and how I think my experience can benefit your team:
- **[Skill the ad asks for]:** [A specific time you used it, with the result.]
- **[Second requirement]:** [Evidence.]
- **[Third requirement]:** [Evidence.]

[One sentence on work rights and availability.]

What drew me to this role specifically is [something real about the company or team].

Thank you for considering my application and I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Kind Regards,
[Your name]
